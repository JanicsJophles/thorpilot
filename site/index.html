A companion to your home screen

Cocoon stays
your happy place.

Open Thorpilot from your home screen. Its Android widget now works inside Cocoon, with shortcuts to your workspace, game discovery chat, and requests.

Widget placement and all three shortcuts are tested on AYN Thor. Game-aware tools and in-game assistance are still being designed. Thorpilot is an independent project, not an official Cocoon or AYN product.
